# SEO Growth Strategy - CarnoWash

## Business Reality

CarnoWash is a Persian B2B SaaS product for carwash operators in Iran. The codebase shows real operational modules for vehicle intake, Iranian plate/OCR support, services and pricing, worker assignment, wallet/payments, SMS customer club, attendance, reports, support tickets, tenant registration, and HQ/multi-tenant oversight.

The current public SEO surface is intentionally small: `/login` and `/about/`. That is safe for privacy but not enough for organic growth. Growth should come from new public marketing/resource pages while keeping `/manager/`, `/hq`, `/support`, and `/attendance/` private/noindexed.

## Competitor Content Gap Analysis

Known Persian SERP competitors include broad “نرم افزار جامع مدیریت کارواش” vendors such as Aytak System and adjacent accounting/POS software vendors. They often compete on generic software language.

CarnoWash can differentiate with concrete operational pages:
- AI/plate recognition for Iranian carwash workflows.
- Worker settlement, tips, payroll, insurance and share calculation.
- SMS customer lifecycle and customer club.
- HQ/multi-branch management.
- Real workflow from vehicle entry to release.
- Privacy-aware internal app positioning.

## Required Sections Per Commercial Page

- Problem statement tied to carwash operations.
- Workflow screenshot or diagram.
- Feature bullets grounded in actual product modules.
- Data/privacy note.
- Internal links to related features.
- FAQ-style Q&A for users, not generic filler.
- Demo/contact CTA.

## Topic Clusters

1. Operations: پذیرش خودرو، صف، خدمات، ترخیص، فاکتور.
2. Finance: گزارش درآمد، سهم نیرو، انعام، پرداخت، کیف پول.
3. Customer retention: باشگاه مشتریان، پیامک، گروه بندی، بازگشت مشتری.
4. AI/OCR: پلاک خوان، کاهش خطای تایپ، دوربین ورودی.
5. HR: حضور و غیاب، شیفت، عملکرد نیرو.
6. Multi-branch: HQ، شعبه، نقش ها، پشتیبانی.

## Internal Linking Architecture

- Product landing links to all feature pages.
- Each feature page links back to product, pricing, demo, and one relevant guide.
- Guides link to exactly one primary feature page and the demo page.
- Comparison pages link to pricing, demo, and feature proof pages.
- `/about/` should link to product landing and demo after those pages exist.

## Schema Recommendations

- Product/software pages: `SoftwareApplication`, `WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList`.
- Pricing: `SoftwareApplication`, `Offer`.
- Guides/blog: `Article` or `BlogPosting`.
- Demo/contact: `ContactPage`, `Organization`.
- Local pages: `LocalBusiness` only after verified address/phone/service-area data exists.
- Avoid HowTo schema. FAQPage can help AI parsing, but do not expect Google rich-result eligibility for a commercial software site.

## Local SEO And GBP Plan

CarnoWash should start as a national Persian SaaS site. City pages are only recommended if sales/support coverage is real.

GBP plan if a real office or service-area business profile exists:
- Category: Software company or closest available equivalent.
- Name: CarnoWash.
- Description: focus on carwash management software, not public carwash washing service.
- Add screenshots, logo, and product explanation photos.
- Link GBP to `/software/carwash-management/` or `/demo/`, not `/login`.
- Publish posts about product features and guides.

## Backlink Acquisition Plan

- Partner pages from SMS providers, POS/payment providers, and carwash equipment suppliers.
- Persian SaaS/startup directories.
- Guest content on auto service and small business operations blogs.
- Downloadable assets: checklist for managing carwash operations, worker share calculator template, SMS template pack.
- Case studies from real customers without exposing private tenant data.

## Conversion-Focused SEO Recommendations

- Do not send prospects directly to `/login`.
- Add demo/contact CTA on every public growth page.
- Add screenshots using sanitized data.
- Add plan/pricing clarity or at least “request pricing” flow.
- Add trust proof: deployment model, privacy, support, real modules, and customer story.
